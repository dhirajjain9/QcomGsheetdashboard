🚀 Founder's Launchpad if you were launching a brand here — where to enter & how to win

Reading this section as a founder: Opportunity blends Demand (total sales of the type), White space = √(under-served × beatable-leader) — high when demand is spread over few SKUs (under-served) and the strongest incumbent is beatable. The "beatable" part counts only each brand's organically-earned share — ad-propped share is discounted, since rented visibility fades when budgets drop. High = room to enter.) and Availability gap (how badly incumbents are out of stock — sales-weighted). White spaces — where there's room to enter

White space = √(under-served × beatable-leader) — high when a product type has demand spread over few SKUs (under-served) and no organically-entrenched leader (a leader winning on ad spend is discounted, since rented visibility fades). Availability & stockout loss

OSA = on-shelf availability (% of time in stock). Where demand (Share of Voice) is high but OSA is low, sales are being lost to stockouts. Est. lost is a rough estimate assuming a 75% target availability (capped at 2× current sales). Scope follows the Top-SKUs selector above.

Price Band Analysis

PPU = selling price per single unit, so multipacks compare fairly (a ₹114 pack of 25 candles = ₹5/unit, not ₹114). Bands are derived from the selected product type's own ₹/unit range (not generic), so a tier means "premium for this type". The band with lots of demand but few SKUs is the value white space to attack. Pick a product type; scope follows the Top-SKUs selector.
